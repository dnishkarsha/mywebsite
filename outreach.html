Outreach & leadership

Bringing earthquake engineering to the community

As a founding officer of the Earthquake Engineering Research Institute (EERI) Student Chapter at Utah State, I served as its inaugural secretary and later its president (2024–2025). Together we brought shake tables, geophones and earthquake science to students, families and K–12 classrooms.

Visiting professionals · 2024 & 2025

Bringing practicing engineers to campus

Through EERI's Friedman Family Visiting Professionals Program, I organized two visits as chapter president:

  • Dr. Faiz Makdisi (Gannett Fleming), March 2024: a lecture on the Oroville Dam spillways and tours of the Utah Water Research Laboratory and the SMASH Lab.
  • Dr. John Thornley (WSP), March 2025: a lecture on liquefaction mitigation of coral sand in the Marshall Islands.
Community outreach · 2023–2025

Community Nights and STEM outreach

Every year the chapter ran hands-on earthquake demonstrations for the public: shake tables where kids test their own towers, and a geophone game that asks “How strong can you stomp?”

  • Community Nights in 2023, 2024 and 2025, each drawing 100+ local families and K–12 students
  • STEM Night at Wilson Elementary (2024) and Day on the Quad at USU (2024)
  • Engineering State summer camp for high school students (2023)
Seismic Design Competition · 2025

Starting USU’s first competition team

During my term as president, the chapter put together Utah State’s first team for the EERI Seismic Design Competition. Six undergraduates designed, built and shake-tested a balsa-wood tower and competed at UC Berkeley in 2025, where they were named Rookie of the Year.

Building a student community

Beyond outreach, the chapter connects students with the profession: a resume workshop with Sunrise Engineering, tower-building socials, and talks from visiting engineers.
